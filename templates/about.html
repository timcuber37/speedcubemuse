How SpeedCubeMuse works

What is SpeedCubeMuse?

SpeedCubeMuse helps you explore World Cube Association (WCA) competition results, understand competition rules, and play guessing games about cubers. Ask questions in your own words.

Questions to try

  • What is the world record single for 3x3?
  • Who are the top 10 2x2 solvers by average?
  • How many competitions were held in the United States in 2025?
  • Who has set the most 3x3 single world records?
  • Who won the 3x3 final at the 2023 World Championship?

Tips for asking questions

  • Name the event and result type

    For example, ask for the fastest 3x3 single or average. A single is one solve; an average combines several attempts in a round.

  • Add a place or year when it matters

    Use a country name such as "United States" and include a year if you want results from a particular period.

  • Say which event someone won

    A competition has several events. Try "Who won the 3x3 final?" and include the competition name.

  • Try rewording your question

    If the answer doesn't look right, add a missing detail or ask more simply.

Ask about competition rules

Ask a Delegate is an AI assistant that looks up relevant parts of the WCA Regulations and Guidelines and explains them. Answers include links so you can read the rules yourself. You can ask follow-up questions in the same conversation.

For a decision at a competition, ask the WCA Delegate there.

Questions to try

  • What happens if I touch the puzzle after stopping the timer?
  • When is a two-second (+2) penalty given?
  • Can I use earplugs during a solve?
  • What does DNF (Did Not Finish) mean?

Guess the Cuber

Test your cubing knowledge with three guessing games. Think of a competitor for the app to guess, ask questions to guess the app's competitor, or play against a friend. Choose a difficulty before you start.

Questions use WCA competition results, such as records, rankings, and competition history. Head to head requires both players to sign in.

Signing in

You can try result searches and rules questions as a guest. Sign in with Google or your WCA account to continue after the guest limit, save result questions, and play head to head.

Use SpeedCubeMuse in Discord

Add the bot to your server to ask about results and competition rules in Discord.

/delegate <question> Ask about competition rules, then ask follow-up questions in the reply thread.
/query <question> Ask about competitors, records, and results.
/help See commands and example questions.
/ping Check whether the bot is responding.

Add the bot to your server

Open the SpeedCubeMuse invite link, choose your server, and review the requested permissions. You'll need permission to manage that server. Select Authorize to finish.

In a text channel the bot can access, choose /query and enter a question such as “Who has the fastest 3x3 single?” Use /delegate for a rules question.
