How the fame score works

Records, World Championship medals and high rankings add to a competitor's score. Recent achievements count more, and events have different weights. SpeedCubeMuse uses this score to choose the competitors for each difficulty.

See scoring details

Starting points

6 points per world record 3 points per continental record 2 points per World Championship medal

Single and average records both count. The event and the age of each achievement change how many points it adds.

Event weights

Records, medals and current rankings are multiplied by the event's weight. These weights are SpeedCubeMuse's estimate of how widely each event is followed.

Current world rankings

Each event earns points once, using the better of the competitor's single and average rankings.

Below the top 5000 points

A top-500 ranking can increase a score. To enter this index, a competitor still needs a continental or world record, or a top-100 ranking.

When the achievement happened

Older records and World Championship medals keep a smaller share of their points:

This year100%
5 years ago60%
10 years ago40%
20 years ago25%

The share gradually decreases, but never falls below 20%.

Other contributions

  • Competed this year or last year: 5 extra points. If their latest competition was two or three years ago: 2 extra points.
  • Currently holds a world record: up to 8 extra points, adjusted for the highest-weighted event in which they hold one.
  • More recorded results add a small bonus. Each additional result contributes less as the total grows.

The total is multiplied by 10 and rounded to give the score below. Easy and Normal are based on a competitor's position in this list, so there is no fixed score needed to enter either difficulty.
