Guess the Cuber

How well do you know your cubers? Use clues from World Cube Association (WCA) competition results to guess a competitor. Choose how you'd like to play.

Head to head

Each player picks a secret competitor. Take turns asking questions and guessing each other's competitor. Both players sign in and join with a code.

Choose a difficulty

Start with Easy for 300 well-known competitors. Normal includes 1,000 competitors. Hard includes anyone who has set a continental or world record, or is ranked in the world's top 100 for a single or average in any event.

Everyone includes any competitor in our WCA results in You guess mine. In I'll guess yours and Head to head, it adds competitors with at least five competitions to the Hard selection. More competition history gives the game more clues to work with.
