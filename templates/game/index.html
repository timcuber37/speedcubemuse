Guess the Cuber

A guessing game over the WCA database. Every competitor in the pool has held a continental record or better, or is currently ranked in the world top 100.

Mode 1

I'll guess yours

Think of a competitor and answer yes or no. Usually takes about a dozen questions — and it can recover if you get one wrong.

Play →
Mode 2

You guess mine

I pick a competitor in secret. Ask anything you like in plain English, then name them when you're ready.

Play →
Mode 3

Head to head

You and a friend each pick a competitor and take turns asking. First to name the other's wins. Share a code to start.

Play →

Difficulty

Easy is the 300 most recognizable competitors. Normal widens that to 1,000. Hard is every record holder and current world top 100 — including plenty you may genuinely never have heard of.

Everyone goes further. In you guess mine it can hide literally any WCA competitor, all ~297,000 of them. In the modes where the app has to do the guessing it covers everyone with five or more competitions: below that, over half the database has been to a single competition and looks identical in every attribute there is to ask about.
