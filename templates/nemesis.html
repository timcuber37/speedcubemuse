What counts as a nemesis?

Every comparison includes both singles and averages. A nemesis has a strictly better personal best for every single and average you have. They must also have a result for each of those comparisons.

Ties do not count as wins. Only valid ranked results in current official WCA events count; missing results, DNF and DNS do not create wins. Someone with no valid ranked results cannot nemesize or be nemesized. The reverse comparison uses the other competitor’s results, so the two lists can cover different events.
